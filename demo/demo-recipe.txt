Garlic Parmesan Roasted Potatoes

Ingredients:
- 1.5 lb baby potatoes, halved
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley

Method:
1. Preheat the oven to 425°F and line a sheet pan with parchment.
2. Toss the potatoes with the olive oil, garlic, salt and pepper.
3. Spread them cut-side down on the pan and roast for 20 minutes.
4. Sprinkle with Parmesan and roast 5 to 8 more minutes, until golden.
5. Top with parsley and serve hot.
